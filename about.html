About IQRA

Learning the Quran. Living the Sunnah.

Our Mission

IQRA is an Islamic learning platform dedicated to making the Quran and Sunnah accessible, understandable, and engaging for everyone. Our mission is to help Muslims build a consistent connection with authentic Islamic knowledge.

Our Vision

We envision a global community that learns, understands, and practices Islam with sincerity, knowledge, and wisdom—using modern technology guided by traditional Islamic values.

What We Offer

  • 📖 Quran with translation & tafsir
  • 📝 Interactive Islamic quizzes
  • 📚 Authentic Hadith collections
  • 🎯 Learning goals & progress tracking
  • 🌙 Weekly & daily Islamic reminders
